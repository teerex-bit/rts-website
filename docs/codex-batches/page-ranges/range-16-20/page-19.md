## Exact visible copy

- `BECOMING · PART ONE · LESSON 1 OF 6`
- `Recognize His Presence`
- `God is already here.`
- `The first step in living with God is learning to recognize what is already true—He is with you right now, in this moment, in your ordinary life.`
- `I am with you always, to the very end of the age. MATTHEW 28:20`
- `SCREEN 1 OF 2`; `You Are Already in His Presence`
- `You do not have to find your way into God’s presence.`
- `Many of us have learned to think of God’s presence as something we enter.`
- `We go to church to meet with God.`; `We set aside time to pray.`; `We worship until we “feel His presence.”`; `We become quiet so we can get close to Him.`
- `Those practices can be valuable. But they can also leave us with an unintended assumption:`
- `God is somewhere I need to get to.`
- `Jesus gives us a different picture.`
- `God is not waiting for you at the end of a spiritual exercise. He is with you in the life you are already living.`
- `When you wake up.`; `While you work.`; `During the conversation.`; `In the car.`; `When you’re frustrated.`; `When you’re uncertain.`; `When nothing particularly spiritual seems to be happening.`
- `The first movement of walking with God is not getting closer to Him. It is learning to recognize that He is already near.`
