Panel 1: “The Executive Center of the Person”; “Your will is not what you think, feel, want, or prefer. Your will is where you decide what you will do with all of it.”; “THE WILL IS” — “The capacity to choose. To intend. To consent or refuse. To commit. To act.”; “THE WILL IS NOT” — “Your thoughts. Not wanting something does not mean you have rejected God’s will. Feeling angry does not require choosing anger. Panel 2: “What Moves the Will?”; “Your will makes the choice—but it does not make that choice in a vacuum.”; “THOUGHTS — What do I believe is true? What I believe shapes the reality within which my will chooses. Renewed thinking makes different choices reasonable.”; “FEELINGS — What is moving within me? Feelings provide information and motivation. They are powerful, but they do not deserve automatic authority.”; “DESIRES — What do I want? The will tends to move toward what the person has learned to see as desirable. Formation changes what we see, which changes what we value, which changes what we desire.”; “THE WILL CAN CHOOSE WHAT IS GOOD BEFORE THE REST OF THE PERSON AGREES.”; “Thought: I know forgiveness is the way of Jesus. Feeling: I am angry and hurt. Desire: I want them to pay for what they did. Will: Nevertheless, I choose not to retaliate.”; “At first, obedience may require this kind of decision. But formation goes deeper than fighting alone.”

Panel 3: “When My Will Aligns With God”; “God is not merely teaching you to choose against yourself. He is changing the self from which your choices come.”; “At First — I choose God’s way despite what I want.”; “Increasingly — I begin wanting God’s way.”; “Over Time — God’s way becomes the way I naturally live.”; divided/aligned lists; “Christlikeness is not finally measured by how often you defeat yourself. It is revealed as there becomes less within you that must be defeated.”; “God doesn’t want to eliminate your will. He wants your will freely aligned with His.”; “I delight to do Your will, O my God.” — Psalm 40:8. Footer reads “PREVIOUS: JOIN”; steps 1–5; “NEXT: 2. THE BODY”.

## Composition

80 px white masthead; 270 px dark navy journey rail; warm off-white lesson canvas. A compact title row sits above three equal vertical lesson panels. Each panel uses a numbered navy circle, serif heading, thin copper rule, stacked subcards, and a bottom insight. The footer is a three-part previous/progress/next navigation bar.

## Interactive-looking elements

Top journey links, outlined “Enter a Conversation” button, account circle, four rail stage links, gold completion badges, highlighted Become rail card, support link, previous/next buttons, and five-step progress control.

## Separable assets

Brand tree mark; stage pictograms; thought, heart, flame, person, seed/tree/crown icons. All are simple enough for independent SVG or CSS geometry. No complex photographic raster is present.
