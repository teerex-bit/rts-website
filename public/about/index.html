About Reforming the Soul

A life with God is learned in the life you already have.

Reforming the Soul helps people notice what has shaped them, see what is true, and become more present to God in the ordinary moments where formation is already happening.

Who we are

We are learning this life with God too.

Reforming the Soul grew from a shared conviction that spiritual formation is not an idea to study from a distance. It is the slow, honest work of letting God reshape the way we see, respond, choose, and live.

We created this space for people who want to understand what is happening within them, find language for what can be difficult to name, and learn to walk with God in the middle of real life.

We are not offering a polished image of spiritual success. We are inviting people into attention, truth, surrender, and practice—the same journey we continue to live ourselves.

What we believe

God does not merely change what you do. He reforms the person from whom your life flows.

Why this work exists

More than information about God.

It is possible to know a great deal about God and still live from fear, control, performance, old wounds, and habits we barely notice. Spiritual formation reaches beneath behavior to the person from whom the behavior comes.

The goal is not to manufacture a better religious version of ourselves. It is to become increasingly able to receive the life God is already offering and respond to Him with freedom.
