God Already Sees It

You may not understand why that reaction is there.

You may not even be seeing it accurately yet.

But God already sees what is happening inside you.

Spiritual formation isn’t about analyzing yourself better.

It’s about learning to see yourself with God and allowing Him to show you what He sees.

A Simple Practice to Begin

When you notice something inside you, try this.

1

Notice

What just happened inside me?

Be honest and specific.
2

Ask

God, what do You want me to see?

Invite Him to show you.
3

Listen

Pay attention to what comes to mind.

It may be a thought, a memory, or a conviction.
4

Receive

Don’t force an answer. Receive what He gives you.

Even if nothing comes.

Some things will become clear right away. Others may take time. Some you may never fully understand. That’s okay.

You don’t have to understand yourself before God can transform you.

You only need to become willing to let Him show you what He sees.

God’s Perspective

“Search me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts.”— Psalm 139:23

Why This Matters

  • You were formed long before you knew you were being formed.
  • God already sees the patterns you are just beginning to notice.
  • He is not surprised by what you see. He wants to help you be free.

In This Lesson You Will

  • Begin to notice what happens inside you
  • See that you have already been formed
  • Invite God to show you what He sees
