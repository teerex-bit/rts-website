Those Reactions Came From Somewhere

The reactions you just noticed didn’t appear from nowhere.

They were learned, reinforced, absorbed, or developed over time.

You have been formed.

Let’s Explore a Little Deeper

Think of one reaction you noticed in the last step.

Where might the reaction have learned to make sense?

Select any that feel true. (Choose as many as you like.)

I tend to…(Example: I need to fix things when things go wrong.)

You don’t have to know where everything came from.

For now, recognize this:

Your reactions have a history. You have been formed.
