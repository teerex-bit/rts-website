Awaken 1 • Lesson 1 of 7

Catch Yourself Being You

For the next day, don’t try to change yourself.

Just live your normal life and pay attention.

The goal isn’t to become more spiritual.

It’s to notice what is already happening inside you so you can invite God into it.

Your Practice

When something gets your attention, use this simple rhythm:

1

Notice

What just happened inside me?

Be honest and specific.
2

Ask

God, what do You want me to see?

Invite Him to show you.
3

Listen

Pay attention without trying to manufacture an answer.

It may be a thought, a memory, or a conviction.
4

Receive

Take whatever becomes clear and leave what doesn’t.

Even if nothing comes.

I Noticed Something

Use this simple tool to record a moment if you want to.

It’s optional. Use it when it helps.

▣   Your notes are private and only visible to you.

There is no right or wrong here.

You are learning to pay attention and partner with God.

Why This Practice?

  • You can’t change what you aren’t aware of.
  • God often speaks in the ordinary moments of life.
  • Attention is the first step of transformation.

Tips for Success

  • You only need a few moments at a time.
  • It might happen at work, at home, in traffic, or during a conversation.
  • There is no hurry. Just be available.
  • Perfection is not the goal. Presence is.

Remember

God already sees everything inside you. You are simply learning to see it with Him.
