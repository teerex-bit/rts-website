Where Did That Come From?

Before we learn anything new, let’s notice something together.
During an ordinary day, many things happen inside us before we consciously decide what to do.

▣   Let’s notice

Read each situation below. Pause for a moment and picture it in your own life.
What might be happening inside you in that moment?

Why we start here

Spiritual formation begins in the hidden places of life—long before it shows outwardly.

God already sees what is happening inside you. Together, we are learning to see it too.

Everyone’s spirit has already been formed. Its present character has come to be through the experiences and choices of a lifetime.
— Dallas Willard

In this lesson you will

◉  Begin to notice what happens inside you

♟  See that you have already been formed

❧  Prepare your heart for what God wants to show you
