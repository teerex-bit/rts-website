You Will Know the Tree by Its Fruit

Fruit is not the behavior you are trying to produce. Fruit is the evidence of the life that is actually growing within you.

We can imitate patience. Suppress anger. Make ourselves say something kind. We can behave generously while resenting the person we’re helping. These actions may be important acts of obedience during formation—but behavior alone doesn’t tell us what has happened to the person.

“

“A good tree cannot bear bad fruit, and a bad tree cannot bear good fruit.” — Matthew 7:18

The real question

What kind of tree is this becoming?

  • Galatians 5 describes the fruit of the Spirit: Love · Joy · Peace · Patience · Kindness · Goodness · Faithfulness · Gentleness · Self-control
  • These are not nine behaviors God has given you to manufacture. They are increasingly characteristic qualities of a life governed by God’s Spirit.

The diagnostic question

  • What is beginning to come out of me naturally?

Fruit does not mean…

  • You never struggle.
  • Every response is Christlike.
  • Formation is finished.

Fruit means the direction and increasingly natural character of your life are changing. The root is growing. The tree is changing. The fruit is beginning to show.

♡

This is not a quick fix. It is a lifelong formation.

God is patient with the process. He cares more about what you are becoming than about how quickly it happens.
