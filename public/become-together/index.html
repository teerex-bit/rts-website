Become • Part Two

Become Through Life With God

Learning to recognize and cooperate with what life with God is forming in you.

As you continue to live with God—recognizing His presence, listening, releasing control, receiving the moment, and taking the next right step—He is forming you from the inside out.

This part will help you recognize the areas of your life where that transformation happens, and how to cooperate with it.

The five areas of formation

  1. 1. The Will(Thoughts, Desires, Feelings, Choices)

    See how God is renewing your inner life—what you think, what you want, what you feel, and the choices you make.

    2 screens

  2. 2. The Body

    Discover how your body can be trained to support the life God is forming and become an ally, not an obstacle.

    1 screen

  3. 3. Relationships

    Learn how your relationships reveal what is growing in you and become a place where God continues to form your character.

    1 screen

  4. 4. The Soul

    Explore how all of you—mind, will, body, and relationships—begin to work together in increasing wholeness under God’s leadership.

    1–2 screens

  5. 5. Fruit(The Evidence of Change)

    See the fruit of a restored soul in everyday life and learn how real change becomes visible through the life you actually live.

    2–3 screens

A Lifetime of Becoming

Becoming is not a destination. It is a lifelong journey of cooperation with God as He renews every part of you. The more you live with Him, the more His life flows through you—naturally and freely.
