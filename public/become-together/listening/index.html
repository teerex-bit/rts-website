The Body

Training What Has Become Automatic

Your body has learned how you live. Much of what your body does happens before your conscious mind has time to decide.

“

Why we practiced in Part One: Recognizing His presence, listening, releasing control, receiving the moment, taking the next right step, and repeating—these practices train your whole person, including your body. What begins as an intentional practice can become an embodied way of being.

↻

Your body has learned responses that happen automatically.

  1. Challenge

    Someone challenges you and your muscles tense.

  2. Threat

    You feel threatened and your breathing changes.

  3. Irritation

    You become irritated and your voice or face reacts first.

  4. Relief

    You are uncomfortable and reach for relief.

  5. Fatigue

    You are tired and patience disappears.

These responses did not appear from nowhere.

They are the result of repetition. Your body has been formed by the life you have repeatedly lived.

Jesus lived through His body

Jesus’ hands touched people others avoided. His feet carried Him toward those in need. His eyes saw what others overlooked. His mouth spoke truth with love. His body rested, worked, wept, ate, and remained present—even under extraordinary pressure.

What came through His body revealed the person He was.

✋

The goal is not control. It is cooperation.

We are not trying to dominate or punish the body. The goal is an integrated person:

  • Thought increasingly agrees with truth.
  • Feeling increasingly responds to reality.
  • Desire increasingly moves toward what is good.
  • Will increasingly chooses God’s way.
  • Body increasingly cooperates with that choice.

We are teaching the body that it is safe to live under God’s loving leadership.

⌕

Your body can reveal what has been formed

When something goes wrong, notice:

  • What happened to my breathing?
  • What happened to my face or voice?
  • Did I move toward or away?
  • Did I tense, defend, control, or escape?
  • Did I reach for comfort?

Don’t condemn the response—notice it. It may be showing you what has actually been formed inside.

♡

Christlike character is becoming embodied

A changed first response

  • Your first response increasingly resembles the response you once had to choose deliberately.

What others encounter

  • Other people cannot see your renewed thoughts or surrendered desires. They experience them through your body, your presence, your actions, and the way you respond to them.
