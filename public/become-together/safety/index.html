Becoming · Part Three · Lesson 3 of 5

Relationships

What Comes Out of You Toward Others

Relationships reveal what solitude can conceal. It is possible to feel patient when no one is irritating you. To feel humble when no one is challenging you. To believe you have surrendered control when no one is resisting what you want. Then another person enters the situation.

People do not create everything that comes out of us. Very often, they reveal what was already there.

“

“I delight to do Your will, O my God.” — Psalm 40:8. Life with God changes the source from which we relate to others.

♡

I become free to love you without needing to control what I receive from you.

Freedom received

  • When I increasingly receive my security, identity, acceptance, and sufficiency from God, I become free to love you without demanding that you provide them.

Freedom offered

  • This freedom transforms pressure into presence, neediness into generosity, and self-protection into trust that the God who is always enough is still with me.

The Other Person Is Not the Problem I Need to Solve.

○

Disagree with me?

Can I remain present without needing to win?

○

Misunderstand me?

Can I explain myself without demanding that they understand?

○

Disappoint me?

Can I acknowledge the hurt without making them responsible for my peace?

○

Succeed in ways I don’t?

Can I celebrate them without comparing their life to mine?

○

Criticize me?

Can I listen for truth without immediately defending myself?

†

Jesus Changes the Question.

Our natural relational question is often: “What are they doing to me?” Formation introduces another: “Who am I becoming toward them?”

Love is not always giving another person what they want. Love is remaining committed to what is genuinely good while refusing to become unlike Christ in the process.

♧

Relationships Become Evidence.

Notice what is actually coming out of you toward this person.

Thoughts
What story am I telling myself about them?
Feelings
What is rising in me?
Desires
What do I want from them right now?
Will
What am I choosing toward them?
Body
What is my face, voice, posture, and presence communicating?
Relationship
What is actually coming out of me toward this person?
☆

A Restored Relational Life Begins to Look Different

Less need to be right.
→More willingness to understand.
Less defensiveness.
→More ability to receive correction.
Less condemnation.
→More discernment without contempt.
Less manipulation.
→More honesty.
Less control.
→More freedom for the other person.
Less dependence on approval.
→More security in God.
Less retaliation.
→More capacity to seek another’s good.
Less keeping score.
→More readiness to forgive.
Less fear of difficult conversations.
→More ability to speak truth in love.
Greater love does not mean weaker boundaries.

A person increasingly secure in God may actually become more capable of saying no.

♧

One of the clearest measures of formation

It is not how spiritual you feel in God’s presence, but what kind of person other people encounter after you have been there. Life with God does not merely teach us how to endure other people. It increasingly frees us to love them—not because everyone becomes easier to love, but because we are becoming increasingly free to love.
