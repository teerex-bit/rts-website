Becoming · Part Four · Lesson 4 of 5

The Soul

The Whole Person Coming Together Under God

A soul is not one more compartment in us. It is what integrates us into one life.

The question is not, “Am I trying harder?” The question is, “Is my whole life becoming one under God?”

“

“And may the God of peace sanctify you completely; and may your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.” — 1 Thessalonians 5:23

♢

The problem: A divided life

Different parts of me often operate from different realities.

  • I believe one thing but feel another.
  • I say I trust God but live in control.
  • I forgive but privately rehearse the hurt.
  • I worship God but satisfy hidden desires.
  • I live one way in public and another in private.

A divided soul is a life in which different parts of me are living from different versions of reality.

◌

The work: Truth reaches the whole person

As I see and believe what is true, it begins to reshape every part of me.

  • The mind sees truth and stops believing lies.
  • The emotions increasingly correspond to reality.
  • Desires are reordered around what is genuinely good.
  • The will increasingly chooses God’s way.
  • The body learns that way of living.
  • Relationships begin expressing it.

Truth is not just known. It becomes lived.

∞

The result: Congruity

Wholeness looks like congruity.

  • What I believe, feel, desire, choose, say, and do increasingly agree.
  • My inner life and outer life are no longer at war.
  • I do not have to maintain multiple versions of myself.
  • Integrity grows: one person in every room, in every situation, in every season.

Maturity is increasing agreement between the truth I profess and the life I actually live.

⌂

The destination: A soul at home in God

A soul at home in God is not perfect. But it increasingly rests in His reality.

  • God is here. God is good.
  • I belong to Him. I can trust Him.
  • I can receive this moment.
  • I can do what He gives me to do.

The inner war quiets because the whole person is coming under His loving rule.

†

What restoration looks like: the whole person increasingly ordered under God

  1. Thoughts

    I increasingly think from what is true.

  2. Feelings

    My emotions increasingly correspond to reality rather than falsehood.

  3. Desires

    I increasingly want what is genuinely good.

  4. Will

    I increasingly choose God’s way freely.

  5. Body

    My automatic responses increasingly cooperate with that choice.

  6. Relationships

    What comes out of me toward others increasingly resembles Christ.

  7. Soul / life under God

    All of me is increasingly one life under His loving rule.

Integrity: the gaps begin to close

Who God sees

  • I am known and loved by Him.

Who I know myself to be

  • I am becoming honest with myself.

Who the people closest to me experience

  • I am becoming consistently who I am.

Who everyone else sees

  • I am not performing; I am becoming.
Integrity is not merely telling the truth.

It is becoming one person everywhere. The secret and public versions of me increasingly become the same person.

♡

Wholeness is not never being disturbed.

It is no longer becoming divided from God, truth, or yourself because you are disturbed. Whatever I experience can now remain connected to God and truth. Grief is not hopelessness. Anger is not hatred. Fear is not control. Pain is not bitterness. Desire is not demand. Disappointment is not distrust of God. Jesus experienced every real emotion, yet remained fully connected to the Father and fully aligned with truth.
