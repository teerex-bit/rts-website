From Devotion, Not Duty

Obedience is not how you earn relationship with God.Obedience is what relationship with God begins producing.

You don’t obey to be loved. You obey because you are loved.

You respond to a Person you have learned to trust.

Desire → Intention → Obedience

  1. DesireI want to live God’s way.

    →

  2. IntentionI have decided to live God’s way.

    →

  3. ObedienceI take the step He gives me.

The surrendered life settles the question of obedience before the instruction comes.

Obedience also teaches us to hear.

As we act on what God shows us, we gain experience with Him.

We discover His faithfulness. We learn His ways.

We recognize His character. We see what His direction produces.

Obedience deepens relationship.

Waiting can be obedience.

Sometimes the next right step is to wait. Not out of fear, but because God is still preparing, aligning, or revealing.

You don’t have to know tomorrow today.

Today’s obedience does not require tomorrow’s information.

God is responsible for where the path leads.

You are responsible for the step He has given you.

The repeatable pattern of walking with Jesus

This is not a one-time process.

It is a rhythm of life.

  1. RecognizeGod is here.

  2. ListenI become attentive to Him.

  3. ReleaseI give up what isn’t mine to control.

  4. ReceiveI accept the moment I actually have.

  5. RespondI take the next right step He gives me.

Then tomorrow,we begin again.
You don’t have to live tomorrow today.You only have to be faithful with the step God has placed in front of you.
