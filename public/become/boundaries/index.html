Living With Open Hands

Surrender isn’t something you settle once. It becomes the posture from which you live, moment by moment.

You may have moments of profound surrender when you wholeheartedly give your life to God. But tomorrow you’ll face another decision, another desire, another fear. Control will offer itself again. This is why surrender is not an event—it is a daily way of life.

CONTROL OFTEN HIDES BEHIND REASONABLE THINGS.

Control can sound like wisdom, responsibility, or even faith. Listen for the underlying posture.

It may sound like:What it reveals:

“I just need to figure this out.”

I cannot rest until I understand everything.

“I need to know what God’s going to do.”

I cannot trust unless I see the outcome.

“I need to make sure this works.”

I cannot be secure unless I control it.

“I can’t let that happen.”

I cannot accept what I cannot prevent.

“What if I make the wrong decision?”

I cannot move forward unless I’m certain.

“I need an answer before I can have peace.”

I cannot have peace until reality agrees with me.

SURRENDER ALLOWS ME TO HAVE PEACE BEFORE I KNOW THE OUTCOME

because my confidence has moved from the outcome to God.

THE PRACTICE: OPEN HANDS

When you notice yourself gripping an outcome, stop and bring it to God honestly.

  1. 1

    Name it honestly. — Father, this is what I want.

  2. 2

    Name the fear. — This is what I’m afraid will happen.

  3. 3

    Name the control. — This is what I’m trying to control.

  4. 4

    Offer it to Him. — But I want You more than I need this to happen my way.

  5. 5

    Invite His leading. — Show me what is mine to do. I will leave what is Yours with You.

  • Release what isn’t yours to control.
  • Receive what God has actually given you.
  • Do what is yours to do.
  • Trust Him with the rest.
This is surrender, not passivity.

GOD IS FORMING A SURRENDERED PERSON

God isn’t only asking you to surrender the situation—He is forming a surrendered person. Over time, as you release control again and again, you begin to learn:

  • I don’t have to control everything.
  • I don’t have to understand everything.
  • I don’t have to secure every outcome.
  • I don’t have to get what I want.
  • I can trust God and remain responsive to Him.
“

Self-rule and life with God cannot occupy the center simultaneously.

Whole surrender makes room for God to be Lord of all.

WHY SURRENDER IS REASONABLE

Surrender is not blind. It is a response to the One we are learning to know.

  • Because God is good, loving, and wise, His ways are safer than ours.
  • Because God sees what we cannot see, His wisdom is greater than ours.
  • Because God keeps His promises, His character is more dependable than ours.
  • Because God is for our good, His direction is better than our preferences.

Surrender is not weakness. It is the strength to trust God with everything—because you trust who He is more than what you want.

Today, choose to live with open hands.
