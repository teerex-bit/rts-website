Even Here, God Is Forming Me

If God has allowed this moment to reach me, then this moment cannot prevent Him from forming me through it.

God is not limited by what happens in this world. His ability to form Christ in you is not stopped by pain, confusion, injustice, loss, or failure.

Romans 8:28“And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose.”This is not a promise that everything is good. It is a promise that God is able.

Many things may be true about this moment.

  • The enemy may intend destruction.
  • Another person may intend harm.
  • A broken world may produce suffering.
  • My own choices may bring painful consequences.
  • Something may happen that I cannot explain.

None of those things removes God from the moment.

None of them means His formative work must stop until circumstances improve.

Instead of only asking…

“Why is this happening to me?”

→

We also begin learning to ask…

  • Father, where are You in this?
  • What is this exposing in me?
  • What do You want me to see that I could not see before?
  • What are You asking me to release?
  • What can You form in me here?
  • What is mine to do now?

You do not have to call the moment good to believe God can bring good from it.

  • You don’t have to understand why He allowed it.
  • You don’t have to pretend it doesn’t hurt.
  • You don’t have to know how the story ends.
  • You only need to know that God has not surrendered this moment simply because you would never have chosen it.

Peter’s story is our story.

Peter’s denial exposed what he did not yet see in himself.

His self-reliance collapsed.

His understanding of his own strength changed.

His dependence on Jesus became deeper.

His experience of grace became real.

The painful moment exposed what the comfortable moment allowed to remain hidden.

This moment may not be what you wanted — but it can still become part of His purpose.

God can meet you here.He is not distant from your pain.

God can lead you here.He can give wisdom, strength, and direction.

God can love you here.His love is not withdrawn in suffering.

God can reveal what is hidden here.What you could not see before may be what He wants to show you now.

God can form Christ in you here.This is the heart of Becoming.

This moment can be redeemed, not because it was good, but because He is good and He is here.
