Even This Moment

Receiving the moment is easy to misunderstand when the moment hurts.

When pain, loss, or betrayal arrives, our first reaction is often confusion and sometimes anger. It is human. But it is not the whole story.

Receiving a painful moment does not mean:

  • God wanted the evil that happened.
  • God is the author of everything.
  • Everything that happens is good.
  • We should remain in preventable harm.
  • We should not grieve, resist evil, seek healing, or pursue justice.
Receiving the moment means accepting that this is the reality in which I must now live with God. It is the place of life He has allowed me to inhabit right now.

Job 1–2: God sets the boundaries

Satan desired to harm Job, but he was not sovereign over Job. God set the limits.

“Everything he has is in your power, but on the man himself do not lay a finger.” — Job 1:12
“He is in your hands; but you must spare his life.” — Job 2:6

The enemy can ask, but he cannot act outside the boundaries God establishes.

Luke 22:31–32: Jesus knows the sifting will come

Jesus speaks to Peter with clarity and compassion.

“Simon, Simon, Satan has asked to sift all of you as wheat. But I have prayed for you, Simon, that your faith may not fail.” — Luke 22:31–32

Notice what Jesus does not say. He does not say, “I have made certain this will not happen.”

He does not promise that the pain will be avoided.

Jesus knew the sifting was coming.He did not promise Peter that it would not happen.He promised that Peter would not be abandoned in it.

Then Jesus adds a hope that reaches beyond the painful moment:

“And when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers.” — Luke 22:32

Jesus already sees beyond Peter’s failure. He sees the man Peter will become. Pain is not the end of the story when you belong to Jesus.

The truth we hold onto:

God’s sovereignty does not mean everything that happens is good. It means evil never becomes sovereign simply because the moment is painful. God is still God. He is still in control. He is still at work.

You are not outside His care, even in the moment you never wanted to face.
