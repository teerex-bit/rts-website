What Is Mine to Do?

You do not need to know the whole path to faithfully take the step God has given you.

We want to know where this is going. How it will work. What happens next.

But God often reveals the next step—not the whole journey.

His way is not: clarity first, then obedience.

It is: enough light for the next step, then trust and response.

The next right step is usually smaller than the future you’re trying to solve.

Make the call. Apologize. Wait. Say yes.

Say no. Tell the truth. Forgive. Give.

Ask. Rest. Finish what you know to do.

Or simply—do nothing yet.

Waiting can be obedience.

God is responsible for the path.

He knows where it leads.He sees the whole picture.He provides what is needed.

You are responsible for the step.

You cannot control the path.But you can be faithful with the step He gives you.

Biblical pattern

  • Abraham was told to go before he knew exactly where.
  • Israel received enough manna for just one day.
  • The priests stepped toward the Jordan before it parted.
  • Peter stepped out of the boat on one invitation: “Come.”
  • The disciples were called with incomplete instruction: “Follow me.”
God reveals enough →You trust → You respond →More becomes visible.

The practical question

Father, what is mine to do right now?

Not what will fix everything.

Not how to make this turn out.

What is mine to do in this moment?

  • Today’s obedience does not require tomorrow’s information.
  • I don’t need to know how Step 7 will work while He is asking me to take Step 1.
  • I trust Him with what I cannot see.
  • I take the step He has given me.
