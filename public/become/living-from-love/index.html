Learn to Live From His Presence

Recognizing God’s presence should change what happens next.

Most of us live from whatever captures our attention. But when we recognize that God is already here, we can choose a different first response.

The Practice

Don’t try to think about God every second today. You will become distracted.

Learn to return.

When you notice you’ve become absorbed in your thoughts, plans, frustration, fear, or busyness—return to His presence.

No guilt.
No elaborate prayer.
Just return.

You are here.
I am here.
What are we doing?

Old Pattern

We react from what we see.

  1. A problem appears.
  2. We think.
  3. We worry.
  4. We protect or control.
  5. We imagine outcomes.
  6. We react.
  7. And maybe eventually we remember God.
→

A New First Response

We respond from who is already here.

  1. 1Stop for a moment.
  2. 2God is here.
  3. 3Father, what are You seeing that I am not seeing?
  4. 4What are You doing here?
  5. 5Is there something You want me to do?
  6. ♥Then, continue with peace and purpose.
“

The goal isn’t to become someone who spends all day thinking about God.
The goal is to become someone who increasingly lives with God.
