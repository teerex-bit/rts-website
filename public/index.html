You’re being shaped
by something.

Do you know what it is?

Your experiences, culture, relationships, fears, desires, and beliefs—even what you’ve been told about God—shape the way you see the world.

But are they shaping you into who God created you to be?

Reforming the Soul is about learning to see clearly, live with God daily, become like Him, and join Him in what He’s doing.

A journey of formation

Growth happens in a sequence.

Awaken

Become aware of what has shaped you.

Discover the influences, assumptions, and patterns that have formed the way you see, think, and respond.

→

See Clearly

Learn to see differently.

Examine truth. Separate assumptions from reality. See God as He truly is.

→

Become

Allow His life to transform yours.

His life changes you from the inside out—your character, desires, and perspective.

→

Join

Participate in what God is doing.

Recognize His work and respond to His invitation.

Explore resources to support your journey.
