What Actually Happened?

The New Testament is very clear about what happened when you trusted in Jesus.

Let’s look at the three-part picture.

Old Person

Crucified with Christ

Your old identity—ruled by sin and separated from God—was crucified with Christ.

Our old self was crucified with him so that the body ruled by sin might be done away with.
Romans 6:6
→

Old Formation

Patterns that remain

Habits, beliefs, emotions, and ways of seeing were formed in the old life.

Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.
Romans 12:2
→

New Creation

The person now alive to God

God has made you new on the inside—alive to Him and united with Christ.

If anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!
2 Corinthians 5:17

You are not who you used to be.

You still have things to unlearn and renew.

You are learning to live from who you already are in Christ.

Identity is settled. Formation is the journey.

You don’t need to become new. You need to live from what is already true.

Why This Matters

When you confuse old formation with your true identity, you live in unnecessary defeat and shame.

When you see what is true, you can walk in the freedom of your new life.

In This Lesson You Will

  • See what the New Testament says actually happened to you.
  • Understand the difference between identity and formation.
  • Recognize that your real identity is secure in Christ.

Remember

Truth seen clearly becomes a foundation for life change.
