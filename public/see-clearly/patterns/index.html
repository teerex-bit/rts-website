What Are You Still Living From?

Even though you are a new creation, old formation can still influence the way you think, feel, desire, choose, react, and live.

Let’s take an honest look together.

For each area below, choose the statement that most often describes you right now.

There are no right or wrong answers—this is for your discovery.

Old Formation May Say…

New Life Can Learn…

When I fail, I feel guilty and condemned. I need to make things right.

→

When I fail, I can receive grace and run to the Father who loves me.

I need to earn God’s favor by doing more and trying harder.

→

I can receive from God. His acceptance is a gift, not a reward.

I protect myself and keep control so I won’t get hurt or let anyone down.

→

I can surrender and trust God. He is safe, good, and always for me.

I have to figure it out and handle it on my own.

→

I can depend on God. His strength is made perfect in my weakness.

This is just who I am. I’ll probably never change much.

→

God is forming me into someone new. I can become more like Jesus.

Awareness is the first step toward freedom.

You can’t change what you won’t acknowledge.

Why This Matters

What you’re still living from is not evidence that the old person is alive. It’s evidence of formation—patterns that can be renewed.

The goal is not to fix yourself. The goal is to learn to live from the new life within you.

In This Lesson You Will

  • Identify patterns of old formation that still influence your life.
  • Compare them with the new life you can learn to live from.
  • Begin to distinguish between instincts formed in the past and truth that sets you free.

Remember

You are not what your instincts say. You are who God says you are.
