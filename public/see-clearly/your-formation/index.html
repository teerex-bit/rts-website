Who Are You Now?

Before we talk about beliefs and assumptions, we need to start with something more foundational—your identity.

The way you see yourself shapes everything else.

Let’s Be Honest

Which statement sounds most like how you actually think about yourself?

Don’t choose what you think you’re supposed to believe.

Choose what most closely describes how you really see yourself.

I’m a sinner trying to become more like Jesus.

I’m a sinner saved by grace, and I’ll always struggle with sin.

God has forgiven me, but fundamentally I’m still the same person trying to do better.

Something else. Tell us how you would describe it.

Let’s look again—together—at what the New Testament actually says about who you are in Christ.

The answer might be different from what you’ve been taught.

Why This Matters

If you see yourself primarily as a sinner trying to improve, you will live from effort and fear.

But if you see yourself as God sees you, it changes how you think, feel, and respond.

Foundational Realities

  • You are a new creation. The old person died with Christ.
  • God is good, loving, and committed to your good.

In This Lesson You Will

  • Discover the truth about your identity in Christ.
  • Learn what it means that you are a new creation.
  • Begin to see the difference between old formation and new life.
