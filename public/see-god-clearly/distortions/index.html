Reflection 5 of 6

The Same God.

Jesus does not reveal a different God from the Old Testament. He reveals clearly the God who was there all along.Look at the heart of God in both Testaments.What do we see?

In Jesus (New Testament)

In God (Old Testament)

Compassion

Jesus was moved with compassion toward suffering people. (Matthew 9:36)

God saw their suffering and said, “I am concerned about their suffering.” (Exodus 3:7)

Mercy

Jesus welcomed sinners and offered forgiveness and new life. (Luke 15:1–7)

“Merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love.” (Exodus 34:6–7)

Faithfulness

Jesus remained faithful—even when His followers failed Him. (2 Timothy 2:13)

God’s faithfulness continues through Israel’s failures. (Lamentations 3:22–23)

Kindness

Jesus provided, healed, and gave good gifts. (Matthew 7:11)

“The Lord is good to all, and His mercy is over all that He has made.” (Psalm 145:9)

Patience

Jesus patiently taught and endured slow, confused disciples. (Mark 4:33–34)

God bore with Israel again and again for many generations. (Nehemiah 9:28–31)
