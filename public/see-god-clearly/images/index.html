Is This God Trustworthy?

You’ve seen His heart in both Testaments.You’ve compared what you’ve heard with what Scripture says.You’ve looked at Jesus, the clearest revelation of the Father.Now the question is personal.

Take a moment to reflect:

  • Does His character consistently reflect what is good, true, just, and life-giving?
  • Does He keep His promises—even when I don’t understand the timing?
  • Do I believe He is for my ultimate good and is able to bring good from all things?
  • Can I entrust my life to Him?

He is good. He is faithful. He is just. He is kind. He is holy.And He is worthy of your trust.
