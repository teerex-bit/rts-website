Reflection 5 of 6

Look Again.

Some things we’ve heard
just aren’t what Scripture says.

You’ve heard it said:

“The God of the Old Testament was angry. Jesus showed us a God of love.”

→
But Scripture shows:

The same God shows both justice and love throughout Scripture. Jesus reveals His heart most clearly.

(John 14:9; Malachi 3:6)
You’ve heard it said:

“God’s judgments mean He has changed His mind about me.”

→
But Scripture shows:

God’s discipline is never to destroy—it is to correct, restore, and bring life.

(Hebrews 12:5–11; Hosea 11:8–9)
You’ve heard it said:

“If God is good, why is there so much hardship and pain?”

→
But Scripture shows:

Scripture shows a world affected by sin, and a God who is working all things toward healing and renewal.

(Romans 8:18–28; Isaiah 61:1–3)

Don’t build your picture of God on tradition or a few difficult passages.
Let the whole of Scripture—culminating in Jesus—shape your understanding.
