What About God’s Anger?

God’s anger is not the opposite of His love.
It is love that opposes what destroys the people He loves.

Jesus’ Righteous Anger

  • Angry when people were being harmed (Mark 3:5)
  • Angry at injustice and exploitation in God’s house (John 2:13–17)
  • Pronounced severe judgment on hypocrisy and hardness of heart (Matthew 23)
  • Sorrowful over sin and rebellion (Luke 19:41–44)

God’s Righteous Anger (Old Testament)

  • Angry against injustice and oppression (Isaiah 5:8; Amos 5:11–12)
  • Angry at idolatry and turning away from Him (Deuteronomy 32:16–21)
  • Angry against wickedness that destroys life (Genesis 6:5–7; Psalm 7:11)
  • Yet He declares His goal: restoration and life, not destruction (Ezekiel 18:23; 33:11)

Love is not indifferent to what destroys.
God’s anger is His commitment to what is good, true, and life-giving.
