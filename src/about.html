About Reforming the Soul

Formation is happening whether we notice it or not.

Reforming the Soul exists to help people pay attention to what is forming them, see themselves and God more clearly, and learn to live with God in the ordinary moments of life.

We are learning this life with God too.

Reforming the Soul grew out of a shared conviction that spiritual formation is not an idea to study from a distance. It is the slow, honest work of letting God reshape the way we see, respond, choose, and live.

We are Trey and Malea. We created this space for people who want to pay attention to what is forming them, find language for what they are experiencing, and learn to walk with God in the middle of real life.

More than information about God.

It is possible to know a great deal about God and still live from fear, control, performance, old wounds, and habits we barely notice. Spiritual formation reaches beneath behavior to the person from whom the behavior comes.

Our work is designed to create room for attention, honesty, surrender, and practice. The goal is not to manufacture a better religious version of ourselves. It is to become increasingly able to receive the life God is already offering and respond to Him with freedom.

The Formation Journey

The core journey moves through four stages. Each stage builds on the one before it without turning growth into a formula.

01

Awaken

Slow down enough to notice what is actually happening within you.

02

See Clearly

Understand the formation, beliefs, images, and assumptions shaping the way you live.

03

Become

Practice life with God until surrender, attention, and love increasingly become natural responses.

04

Join

Carry what God has formed in you into relationships, service, and the places where He is already working.
